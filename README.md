## About Me

I’m an applied **AI / ML Engineer and Data Scientist** with a background in physics and complex systems, focused on building and evaluating **production-oriented machine learning and LLM-based systems**.


Previously, I worked as a **Data Scientist at [Carta Healthcare](https://www.carta.healthcare)**, designing and owning end-to-end ETL and NLP pipelines for clinical data, integrating LLM-based extraction workflows, and standardizing heterogeneous healthcare data under HL7 and FHIR to support scalable analytics and ML systems.

- PhD in Physics Applied to Medicine and Biology (University of São Paulo – USP)
- Experience working remotely with US-based teams in healthcare and EdTech
- Strong focus on reproducible ML pipelines, evaluation, inference, and system reliability
- Languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese
